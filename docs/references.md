# Research references

VoltCast does not redistribute third-party PDFs. The following publications motivated the model structure and provide starting points for calibration work:

1. Bing Xia, Xin Zhao, Raymond de Callafon, et al. “Accurate Lithium-ion battery parameter estimation with continuous-time system identification methods.” *Applied Energy* 179 (2016): 426-436. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2016.07.005>
2. S. Nejad, D. T. Gladwin, and D. A. Stone. “A systematic review of lumped-parameter equivalent circuit models for real-time estimation of lithium-ion battery states.” *Journal of Power Sources* 316 (2016): 183-196. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2016.03.042>
3. Breno H. M. Neves, Bruna M. J. C. Vilar, Fabiano de M. Silva, et al. “Battery lifetime estimation by means of an analytical continuous-time model.” *INDUSCON* (2016): 1-5.
4. Chiara Caiazza, Valerio Luconi, and Alessio Vecchio. “Energy consumption of smartphones and IoT devices when using different versions of the HTTP protocol.” *Pervasive and Mobile Computing* 97 (2024): 101871. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmcj.2023.101871>
5. Johannes Schmalstieg, Stefan Käbitz, Madeleine Ecker, et al. “A holistic aging model for Li(NiMnCo)O2 based 18650 lithium-ion batteries.” *Journal of Power Sources* 257 (2014): 325-334. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2014.02.012>
6. T. Guena and P. Leblanc. “How depth of discharge affects the cycle life of lithium-metal-polymer batteries.” *INTELEC 06* (2006): 1-8.

References describe prior work; they do not imply that the default VoltCast parameters reproduce a paper’s experimental setup.
